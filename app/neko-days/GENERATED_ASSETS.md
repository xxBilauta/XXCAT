# ZOA 貼紙素材

使用內建 `image_gen`，以使用者提供的 ZO&FRIENDS 圖片為角色參考，生成一張 3×3 透明貼紙圖集。

專案檔案：`dist/assets/zoa-stickers.png`。圖片是依參考圖製作的主題插畫，不標示為官方商品。介面使用 CSS 精靈圖位置顯示每一款貼紙，未再修改生成圖片。橘色英國短毛貓是另外製作的 SVG 介面插畫。

## 最終生成提示詞

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE square transparent sprite atlas for a calendar app sticker picker. Image 1 is the user-supplied ZOA character reference. Create a single production-ready 3 by 3 sprite atlas with EXACTLY 9 separate die-cut sticker graphics, one centered in each of nine equal square cells, evenly spaced. No grid lines, no captions, no letters, no wordmark, no background. Genuine transparent alpha across all empty space. Every sticker must stay within the central 75% of its cell with clear transparent margins so the app can display cells separately using CSS background positions. Keep ZOA highly faithful to reference: chunky shaggy gray cloud cat, tiny ears, enormous half-lidded golden-yellow eyes with black pupils, fluffy white cloud-like brow across both eyes, small pout, pink rounded paw pads, blue ruffled neckerchief, scribbly dark ink outlines and flat muted colors. Top row left to right: 1 seated ZOA with a white daisy growing from head and blue neckerchief; 2 ZOA with blue neckerchief holding a tiny white daisy; 3 curled sleeping ZOA with fluffy gray tail and tiny floating sleep marks (no text). Middle row: 4 ZOA raising both pink paws, front view; 5 ZOA peeking from a small kraft cardboard box; 6 ZOA wearing a small golden crown charm. Bottom row: 7 ZOA cuddling a daisy cushion; 8 ZOA playing with a blue ball of yarn; 9 round ZOA face badge framed by a little white daisy and two tiny pink/yellow bubble friends from reference. Each is a clean charming flat hand-drawn merchandise-style sticker illustration with a thin white die-cut rim, NOT photorealistic products. Reference fidelity matters, this is the same gray ZOA character, not an orange cat. Square composition, consistent equal scale, perfectly regular 3x3 arrangement. Do not include the blue reference background or logos. Render all 9 fully inside the image.
